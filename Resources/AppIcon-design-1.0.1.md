# Time 图标设计

- 版本：1.0.1 / build 2
- 生成方式：内置 imagegen（非 CLI）
- 项目素材：`Resources/AppIcon.png`
- 风格：原创二次元猫耳时钟精灵，月白猫咪、紫色眼睛、蓝紫底色、时钟光环。
- 用途：macOS 应用图标。由 AppKit 高质量缩放生成 16–1024px iconset，再封装为 icns，保留原始透明度。
- 本次为重新设计，没有使用旧绿色图标作为图片输入。

## 最终生成提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: finished macOS app icon for a tiny world-clock app called Time. Primary request: redesign it with a tasteful original Japanese anime / 二次元 sensibility; cute, stylish and extremely readable as a small application icon. Subject: a simple white cat-eared clock spirit, a compact chibi cat face with expressive violet anime eyes, tiny soft blush, and a clean circular clock halo behind its head with just two clock hands pointing to about 10:10. The cat itself is the main silhouette, not a detailed human portrait. Style: polished 2D anime cel illustration with clean confident contours, simple broad shapes, restrained detail, subtle dimensional shading; sophisticated kawaii rather than childish clip art. Color: soft periwinkle and sky blue tile, creamy white character, small warm peach accents, dark indigo lines. Composition: one centered emblem filling most of a macOS rounded-square icon tile, a single tiny four-point star as accent, generous clean separation of face and clock halo. 1024x1024 square asset, front-facing, edge-to-edge crisp. The rounded-square tile must occupy about 90 percent of the canvas with a fully transparent narrow exterior margin. The tile background is opaque periwinkle/blue; transparency only outside the rounded-square tile. No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no mockup, no additional floating accessories, no busy space scene, no glossy 3D plastic, no teal corporate clock.
